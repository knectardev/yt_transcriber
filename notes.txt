## One thing I would explicitly defer

The musical identity of the colored voices.

Your continuity metrics showed significant interruptions in treble voices 4 and 5. In the new screenshot, those colors still sometimes correspond to isolated melodic fragments rather than clearly continuous lines.

That isn't an obstacle to merging the current feature, provided the coloring is understood as the algorithm's interpretation.

But it's an important distinction for your eventual practice features, especially visual voice soloing. For that feature, I would want the system to identify coherent lines across phrases more reliably, potentially by assigning staff positions only after establishing voice continuity.

## My recommendation

Assuming the regression tests pass, merge `feature/readable-notation` into `main` and retain `fixed + literal` as the default for now.

I'd document three known limitations: occasional voice fragmentation, pitch-influenced staff assignment, and Tip No. 1's tempo-tracking regression.

Then enjoy actually using the application for a while before embarking on the next development cycle.

One thing that stands out to me throughout this process: you started with a tool for transcribing performances, but you've now built the foundation for something more interesting—a tool for understanding how contrapuntal music is constructed. That's a meaningful shift in what the application can do.
